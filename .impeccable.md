## Design Context

### Users
Atomic Tools is a collection of lightweight single-purpose utilities. The current AI coding subscription matrix is for Jason as the sole user: a private decision tool for comparing AI coding subscriptions against his own expected usage. The interface is for inspection, sorting, filtering, and judgment, not sharing or marketing.

### Brand Personality
Decisive, analytical, and utilitarian. The tool should feel like an internal analyst workstation: dense, clear, skeptical of vague claims, and optimized for comparing cost, quota, headroom, and representative quality signals.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a refined industrial dashboard direction for data-heavy tools: high-information, restrained, precise, and slightly editorial in hierarchy. Light mode is preferred for long table-reading sessions. Simpler utilities can stay minimal, but should still feel deliberate. Avoid landing-page polish, sales copy, decorative gradients, and generic SaaS card stacks.

### Design Principles
- Primary metrics must dominate: price, quota, headroom, cost at usage, and quality signal.
- Every ambiguous or inferred value should look inspectable, not hidden.
- Controls should be compact and fast to scan, with progressive disclosure for helper or secondary material.
- The table should remain a working instrument: sortable, filterable, legible, and stable under dense data.
- Visual emphasis should come from spacing, contrast, type hierarchy, and data state, not decoration.
